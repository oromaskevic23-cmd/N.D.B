# NDB — Buyer Value and Evidence-Gated Utility

**Project founder and architect:** Alexander Nikolaevich Romaskevich (RomaskevicH)  
**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** design hypothesis, not a live redemption program or investment offer.

## Purpose

NDB is a distinct Polygon token project within the CRYPTO IMPERIAL Ecosystem.
The project name New Digital Bitcoin does not make it Bitcoin or an official
Bitcoin product.

The proposed customer is a person or small business that wants a clearly
defined digital service and an easy way to verify what they are entitled to
receive. A potential use is an optional token-denominated right to redeem a
published, limited service package. This is a proposal; a working redemption
service, actual customer rights and available inventory are not established.

## What exists today

- The Polygon Mainnet contract is publicly identifiable and its source is
  listed as an [Exact Match on Sourcify](https://repo.sourcify.dev/137/0x5F5E3dCa37D12F1bFef21418B066c6ccacbebBE4).
  Source matching is not an independent security audit or a guarantee of value.
- The repository includes a read-only observer for one operator-chosen Polygon
  RPC provider, plus offline tests. It is not a redemption service, wallet,
  live payment processor or evidence of market liquidity.
- Current supply, distribution, privileges, transfer behavior, liquidity,
  exchange listings and purchaser rights require separate, dated verification.
  See [token facts](TOKEN-FACTS.md).

## Proposed service: a verifiable digital-work package

Start with one useful deliverable that IMPERIAL Core can actually produce and
support, for example a narrowly defined review or a customized single-user
workflow application. Before anyone buys a token for this purpose, publish:

1. The exact deliverable, acceptance tests, exclusions, turnaround and support.
2. How entitlement is obtained and verified, the published quote in token and
   ordinary currency, network fees, quote expiry and what happens when the
   token price changes.
3. The authorized issuer, inventory and redemption process, handling of failed
   delivery, refunds, privacy, custody and support contacts.
4. A direct ordinary-payment alternative where legally and operationally
   appropriate, allowing customers to compare the complete cost and risk.

An entitlement checker and service acceptance process must be implemented
and tested separately. Possessing NDB today does not establish a present right
to a particular service. Do not promise fixed token-to-service conversion,
discounts, buybacks or refunds until the issuer can actually honor the terms.

## Why a customer might choose NDB, once proven

- A clearly described digital service can be redeemed using a transparent
  public token identity, if an actual service and entitlement system exist.
- A customer can independently inspect the published contract source and,
  using a trusted RPC, observe basic on-chain contract data.
- The buyer can compare the token route against ordinary payment and choose it
  only when the total service value exceeds fees, volatility and wallet risk.

The existence of a token is not a reason by itself to purchase it. Do not claim
that buying NDB guarantees profit, price appreciation, Bitcoin affiliation,
ownership in IMPERIAL Core, global use, liquidity or future listings.

## Evidence gates before any real-value program

1. Document and independently review the contract, distribution and relevant
   privileged operations using dated block-anchored evidence.
2. Deliver one useful service with a real, independently checked result and
   clear rights; validate costs and customer demand without inventing sales.
3. Prototype entitlement checks and customer acceptance with synthetic data or
   a no-value test environment, including expired offers, replay, wrong chain,
   error handling and lost-access scenarios.
4. Review legal classification, consumer rights, privacy, accounting and
   supported jurisdictions before publishing a token-linked offer.
5. Only after security review and explicit authorization consider a limited
   real-value pilot with transparent disclosures and independent reconciliation.
